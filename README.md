A Solana-based platform where music rights holders (artists, indie creators) tokenize a defined slice of a song's future royalty income — not the audio file, not the copyright. Investors buy fractional tokens representing a proportional claim on that revenue; as royalties come in, they're distributed on-chain, pro-rata, to token holders, who can also resell on a secondary market.

Flow: Creator registers (wallet + self-attested rights) → registers the track (Track PDA, ISRC, declared revenue) → sets tokenization config (% of royalty income tokenized + token supply, priced via a revenue-multiple valuation) → tokens minted (Token-2022) → sold on the marketplace → royalty deposits distributed pro-rata to holders → holders trade freely on a secondary market.
